## 8. Pregnancy-specific facts established

  Mild/moderate reactions: no pathway to harm. Her reactions include **presyncope**, so "hers are
  mild" is not established.
- Incidence 1.6/100,000; UKOSS 37 cases: no infant deaths.
- **Epinephrine not withheld in pregnancy** (ASCIA 2024, EAACI 2021, WAO 2020). Dose unchanged.
- **Positioning: <20 wk flat + legs up; ≥20 wk full left lateral**; manual left uterine displacement
  if supine needed; never sit/stand during a reaction. Target SBP ≥90.
- **Oral food challenge: absolute contraindication.** Skin prick: relative. Blood tests: all fine.
  Endoscopy: strong indication only, T2 preferred.
- **Celiac:** untreated RR — FGR 1.68, stillbirth 1.57, miscarriage 1.35, preterm 1.29, −176 g.
  Treated: no excess. "The risk is not knowing."
- **Nutrition:** no meat + no dairy + egg reactions = removes haem iron, B12, iodine, calcium,
  choline. One cup milk ≈ 57% of pregnancy iodine. Most prenatals lack choline. Ferritin threshold

- Medications: amitriptyline **hold**; colesevelam **drop**; cetirizine/loratadine preferred
