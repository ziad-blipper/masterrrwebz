## Why the change

The investigation began from the premise that the patient had alpha-gal syndrome and needed a cure.
Her actual laboratory results showed:

- **Alpha-gal IgE `<0.10 kU/L`** — below the limit of detection, at diagnosis and a year later
- **Pork IgE 14.40** — her highest value, for a meat she has never eaten
- Lamb 10.20, beef 2.38

An antibody to a never-eaten food cannot arise from eating it. Combined with lifelong cat ownership,
immediate reactions opening with oral itching, and trigger foods that are the two mammalian meats
habitually served under-cooked, the evidence points to **mammalian serum albumin allergy** rather
than alpha-gal syndrome.

**The most important single action, because it has a deadline:** celiac serology — tTG-IgA **with
total serum IgA** — drawn **before** any gluten-free trial. The antibodies decay off gluten within
weeks.
