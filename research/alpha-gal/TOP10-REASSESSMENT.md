# TOP-10 REASSESSMENT — the tests that decide what she is allergic to and what she can eat

*14 October 2026. Written after reading HANDOFF.md in full. Research synthesis, not medical advice.
No new research threads were opened; this is a re-ranking of what the case already contains.*

---

## 0. The headline

**Four changes to the previous list.**

1. **Celiac serology moves from #9 to #1.** It is the only test that delay can destroy, the only one
   with a fetal-outcome consequence (untreated celiac: stillbirth RR 1.57, FGR 1.68; treated: none),
   and the SAAT story means she could drop gluten any day on a naturopath's say-so. Nothing else on
   the list has a deadline.
2. **Three of the previous ten slots (plants, egg, seafood) are now conditional on one unanswered
   question** — *did the new reactions start with itchy mouth and throat within minutes?* Until that
   is answered, those panels are speculative. The handoff's §3 questions do not merely "change the
   ranking"; three of them decide whether a third of the panel should be drawn at all.
3. **A nutrition panel enters the top ten.** She is pregnant after a year with no mammalian meat or
   dairy, now avoiding egg and "plants" too. Ferritin, B12 and vitamin D are diet-management tests in
   the most literal sense: they measure whether the restriction is already causing harm. That is the
   strongest available argument for ending restrictions that the allergy evidence does not support.
4. **MUXF3 (CCD) loses its own slot** and rides inside the plant panel. CCD can contaminate test
   results but cannot explain symptoms, and the reported plant problems are symptoms. Sus s 1 and
   Gal d 2 drop entirely.

**One confound the handoff never names.** Every new "reaction" — egg, plants, seafood — appeared in
September, in early pregnancy. Nausea, dizziness, headache and food aversion are among the commonest
symptoms of the first trimester, and they look exactly like the tail of her real allergic reactions
(itchy mouth → dizziness → nausea → migraine). Her true reactions have a signature: **oropharyngeal
itch within minutes.** A two-day episode of nausea and headache without that onset is, in a pregnant
woman with migraine and a chronic gut disorder, far more likely to be pregnancy plus baseline illness
than three simultaneous new IgE sensitisations to unrelated food families. This does not make the
panels wrong to order; it makes the oral-itch question worth more than the panels.

Fish is her best remaining source of iodine, DHA and complete protein in pregnancy, so a false positive here costs more than almost any other. Calprotectin <50 with normal CRP makes IBD and microscopic colitis unlikely without endoscopy (which is deferred in pregnancy) and supports treating the gut illness as a disorder of gut–brain interaction, where diet restriction is not the treatment. **Ordering gate.** Items 6, 7 and 9 should be drawn only if the corresponding reaction began with
oropharyngeal itch within minutes. Without that, they are fishing, and fishing in a highly restricted
pregnant patient produces false positives that become permanent avoidances. If all three answers are
"no oral itch," the draw is items 1–5, 8 and 10, and the remaining budget goes to nothing — not to
more tests.

**If only one thing is drawn:** item 1. **If only two:** items 1 and 2.

## 2. What was removed, and why

| Dropped | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| **Sus s 1 (pork albumin)** | Not verifiably available at US reference labs. Pork f26 at 14.40 plus a positive Fel d 2 already proves the cross-reactivity; a negative Fel d 2 would not be rescued by Sus s 1. Ask for it by name if offered; do not wait for it. |
| **MUXF3 as a standalone slot** | Interpretive control, not a decision test. Mammals do not make CCD epitopes, so it cannot touch the meat results, and it cannot explain symptoms. Folded into item 7. |
| **Gal d 2 (ovalbumin)** | Adds little over whole egg white plus Gal d 1. Gal d 1 is the one that decides baked egg. |
| **Repeat alpha-gal, SIBO breath test, serum DAO, IgG food panels, broad food IgE panels, TPSAB1 before baseline tryptase** | Unchanged from HANDOFF §9. The IgG food panel warning matters more now: if the "plant reactions" turn out to be a naturopath's IgG panel rather than symptoms, the plant slot collapses entirely. |
| **Acute tryptase, oral challenge, skin prick, endoscopy** | Oral challenge: absolutely contraindicated in pregnancy. Skin prick: relatively contraindicated. Endoscopy: deferred. Acute tryptase stays as a standing instruction (see §3), not a scheduled test. |

## 3. The non-tests, re-ranked against the tests

Three things outrank every item in §1.

1. **Two epinephrine auto-injectors and a written plan**, with the positioning rule for her
   gestational age (flat with legs up before 20 weeks; full left lateral from 20 weeks). Outstanding
   since the first corrected draft. Every reaction she has had meets anaphylaxis criteria. Pregnancy
   makes this more urgent, and the SAAT belief makes a deliberate steak meal plausible.
2. **The oral-itch question, asked once for each of the three new foods.** *"When it happened with
   the egg / the plants / the fish dinner, did it start with an itchy mouth and throat within minutes,
   the way steak does?"* This single question gates items 6, 7 and 9 and is worth more than any of
   them.
3. **A clock-timed diary with one mandatory field: oral itch at onset, yes or no.** Plus what was
   eaten, minutes to first symptom, hours to resolution, and whether anyone else was unwell. Four
   weeks of this would settle Q6 and Q8 more reliably than the seafood panel.

**Standing instruction, not a scheduled test:** if she presents to an emergency department during a
reaction, a serum tryptase drawn within 1–4 hours of onset, compared against item 8, is the one
result that distinguishes a mast-cell reaction from a migraine or baseline flare. Sensitivity is only
~53%, so a normal result proves nothing; a raised one is decisive.

## 4. Which §3 questions actually change the list

Ranked by how much they move the panel, not by the order in HANDOFF §3.

| Question | Effect on the list |
| --- | --- |

| **Q8 — untriggered multi-day episodes per month** | Several a month: the seafood episode explains nothing, item 9 is withdrawn, and the September "reactions" are reclassified as baseline illness plus pregnancy. |
| **Q7 — butter at home** | A clean yes kills the butter hypothesis and lowers item 4 from "could give dairy back" to "confirms dairy is fine." |
| **Q2 — true duration of the gut illness** | Short duration coinciding with bread trouble and supplements raises item 1 and the supplement-effect branch; a decade raises item 10 and DGBI. |
| **Q9 — chicken meat** | Any reaction to chicken makes Gal d 5 in item 6 the most important component on the list; none makes it a formality. |
| **Q3 — supplement list** | Does not change which tests are drawn; makes item 5 interpretable and is a pregnancy safety issue on its own (vitamin A, herbals, porcine pancreatin). |
| **Q1 — gestational age** | Changes nothing about the blood draw (all items are pregnancy-safe at any stage). Changes the emergency positioning rule, NSAID limits, and when a deferred endoscopy could be considered. Still the first question to ask, for safety rather than for test selection. |
| **Q10, Q11, Q12, Q13** | Do not change the top ten. Q13 is a free diagnostic sign for item 10's interpretation. Q11 nudges item 8. Q12 opens Sjögren's, which is outside this list. |

## 5. Ordering notes (carried over, with additions)

- **Fel d 2 standalone** (Quest 93382). Labcorp's reflex cat panel will not run it: whole-cat IgE
  will be below 0.35 after 3–4 cat-free years.
- **Egg yolk separately** (Mayo 82753). No egg-white panel contains Gal d 5.
- **MUXF3** (Mayo 609437) in the same draw as the plant components, never after.
- **Total IgE** in the same draw as everything.
- **Der p 10** must accompany shrimp f24 or a positive will be over-read. **Pen m 2** is the
  heat-labile shrimp component that would fit her pattern; Pen m 1 would not.
- **Baseline tryptase** ≥24 h after any reaction resolves. Antihistamines do not affect it.
- **tTG-IgA** only while she is eating gluten. Total IgA with it; if IgA-deficient, tTG-IgG or DGP-IgG.
- **Prenatal labs may already contain** CBC, ferritin, TSH, vitamin D. Collect the results before
  re-ordering item 5.
- **Do not order** repeat alpha-gal, SIBO breath test, serum DAO, IgG food panels, broad food IgE
  panels, urinary 5-HIAA, provocative OGTT, or TPSAB1 before a baseline tryptase.
- **If Fel d 2 is negative**, the reflex is gelatin bovine c74 (Quest 35352 / Labcorp 602843) and,
  if available, Bos d 13. Do not order these pre-emptively.
