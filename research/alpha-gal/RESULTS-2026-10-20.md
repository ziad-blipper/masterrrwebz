## What is NOT an allergy — the avoid-list just got shorter

### Milk proteins: negative

| | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Cow's milk, whole extract (f2) | 3.24 — class 2 |
| **Alpha-lactalbumin (Bos d 4)** | **<0.10** |
| **Beta-lactoglobulin (Bos d 5)** | **<0.10** |
| **Casein (Bos d 8)** | **0.10** — class 0/1, effectively negative |

**All three major milk proteins are negative.** The whole-milk positive of 3.24 therefore has to be
coming from something *not* on the milk component panel — and the obvious candidate is **Bos d 6,
bovine serum albumin**, which is the exact protein she is allergic to. **⚠️ Bos d 6 (e204) was not
tested in this panel.** Order it (Quest 2612) to close the loop.

**Implication:** her dairy reaction is almost certainly the **albumin cross-reaction**, not milk
allergy. In the published series of 15 cat-albumin-IgE patients, 7 had bovine albumin IgE and **all
15 consumed milk without symptoms**. Heat-treated dairy (UHT, cheese, yogurt) carries less intact
albumin. **This is the finding that could restore dairy — and in pregnancy that means iodine and
calcium.** ⚠️ Not a home experiment: oral challenge is contraindicated in pregnancy. This is the
conversation to have with the allergist.

**She is sensitized to essentially every pollen family at low-to-moderate level, with no single
dominant one.** Two readings, not mutually exclusive:

1. **Genuine broad pollen allergy** → the substrate for **pollen-food allergy syndrome**, which was
   the leading explanation for "reacts to plants." Fits.
2. **A pan-allergen signature.** Low-level positivity across *every* unrelated pollen family is what
   **profilin** (present in all pollens) or **CCD** looks like on a panel. The same test — Quest
   39151 — resolves this.

**Dust mite positive** matters for the seafood question: shrimp tropomyosin is 80–82% identical to
mite tropomyosin, so any shrimp IgE test now needs **Der p 10** alongside or it will be over-read.

Note: no birch (Bet v 1) on this regional panel. Oak (0.43) is the closest Bet v 1-family proxy.
